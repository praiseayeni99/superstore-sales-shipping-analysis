# Sales Performance Analysis – Superstore

## 1. Overview

This section analyzes Superstore sales performance using Power BI. The analysis focuses on overall sales, customer segments, product categories, geographical performance, and monthly sales trends.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2.25M |
| Total Orders | 4,916 |
| Average Order Value | $458 |
| Total Customers | 793 |

## 3. Sales Analysis

### 3.1 Sales by Customer Segment

- Consumer generated the highest sales of approximately $1.147M, contributing 51% of total sales.
- Corporate generated approximately $682K, representing 30%.
- Home Office contributed approximately $423K, representing 19%.

### 3.2 Sales by Product Category

| Category | Sales |
|---|---:|
| Technology | $826K |
| Furniture | $723K |
| Office Supplies | $703K |

Technology recorded the highest sales, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

### 3.3 Monthly Sales Trends

- November recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately $345K.
- December followed with approximately $321K.
- September recorded approximately $300K.
- February recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately $59K.

The monthly trend shows stronger sales performance towards the end of the year.

### 3.4 Geographical Sales Performance

The top-performing states by sales were:

1. California – $446K.
2. New York – $306K.
3. Texas – $169K.
4. Washington – $135K.
5. Pennsylvania – $116K.

California recorded the highest sales among the states displayed in the dashboard.

## 4. Key Business Insights

1. **Customer Segment:** The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales at 51%, making it the leading customer segment.

2. **Product Category:** Technology generated the highest category sales at $826K.

3. **Monthly Performance:** November recorded the highest sales, while February recorded the lowest.

4. **Geographical Performance:** California was the leading state by sales, followed by New York.

## 5. Business Recommendations

1. **Consumer Segment:** Maintain customer engagement and develop targeted marketing campaigns to support the leading sales segment.

2. **Technology Category:** Continue monitoring Technology sales and identify opportunities to strengthen category performance.

3. **Seasonal Sales:** Investigate the factors behind stronger November and December sales and use these findings to improve sales planning.

4. **Geographical Expansion:** Evaluate successful sales strategies in California and New York for potential application in other states.

## 6. Power BI Implementation

The Sales Performance dashboard includes:

- KPI cards for total sales, total orders, average order value, and total customers.
- Sales distribution by customer segment.
- Monthly sales trend visualization.
- Top 10 states by sales.
- Sales by product category.
- Date, region, and sub-category slicers.

The dashboard enables interactive exploration of sales performance across different time periods and business dimensions.
