# Power BI Dashboard – Superstore Analysis

This folder contains the interactive Power BI dashboard developed for the Superstore Sales and Shipping Performance Analysis project.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance
- Total sales, total orders, average order value, and total customers.
- Sales performance by customer segment and product category.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Top 10 states by sales.

### 2. Shipping Performance
- Average shipping days.
- Total orders shipped.
- Fastest and slowest shipping modes.
- Average shipping duration by shipping mode, month, region, and city.

### 3. Sales Overview
- Monthly sales performance.
- Month-over-month (MoM) sales growth.
- Year-to-date (YTD) sales.
- Dynamic KPI cards showing selected reporting periods.

### 4. Sales Insights and Recommendations
- Key sales findings.
- Identification of high- and low-performing areas.
- Business recommendations based on sales trends.

### 5. Shipping Insights and Recommendations
- Shipping performance findings.
- Identification of longer delivery durations.
- Recommendations for improving delivery efficiency.

## Tools and Techniques
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX measures
- Data modelling
- Interactive slicers and visualisations
- Time intelligence analysis
